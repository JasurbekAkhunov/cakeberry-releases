# Cakeberry — delete your account / Hisobni oʻchirish / Удаление аккаунта

App: **Cakeberry** (Google Play developer: Drimble CO).

## In the app (fastest)

1. Open Cakeberry and sign in.
2. Go to **Profile** (Profil / Профиль).
3. Tap **Delete account** (Hisobni oʻchirish / Удалить аккаунт) and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

## Without the app

Write to Telegram **@zarabon1** (https://t.me/zarabon1) with the e-mail of your Google or Apple account and the words "Delete my Cakeberry account". It is deleted within 30 days and you get a confirmation.

## What is deleted and what is kept

- **Deleted:** your sign-in account, profile (name, age, phone, region, survey answer), saved contacts and usage statistics linked to you.
- **Kept:** history of orders you already placed (items, amount, date) for the bakery's accounting, for up to 3 years, without access from the app.
