# Cakeberry — Privacy Policy / Maxfiylik siyosati / Политика конфиденциальности

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Cakeberry is the ordering app of the Cakeberry home bakery in Fergana, Uzbekistan.

## What we collect

- **Google account basics** when you sign in: name, e-mail address, profile photo.
- **Profile you fill in**: full name, age, phone number, region, and how you heard about us.
- **Orders**: items, prices, pickup or delivery time, delivery address and comment you enter.
- **Usage statistics**: which screens are opened and for how long, counted per session without
  advertising identifiers.

## Why

To take and deliver your orders, contact you about them, and improve the menu and the app.
We do not sell data and do not show advertising.

## Where it is stored

Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, region europe-west3). Only you and the bakery staff can read
your profile and orders.

## Telegram

Payment confirmation and questions to the pastry chef open Telegram with a prepared message.
Nothing is sent until you press "Send" in Telegram.

## Your choices

You can browse the menu as a guest without signing in. To delete your account data, write to
Telegram @zarabon1 and it will be removed within 30 days.
